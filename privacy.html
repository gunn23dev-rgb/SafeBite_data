Kısaca

Labelio hesap istemez, reklam göstermez, analitik veya izleme aracı kullanmaz. Alerji ve hassasiyet seçimleriniz, taramalarınız ve geçmişiniz yalnızca telefonunuzda saklanır. Geliştirici olarak sizinle ilgili hiçbir veriyi toplamayız ve sunucularımıza göndermeyiz.

"Başka alfabe?" düğmesi, sizin isteğinizle Google Çeviri'yi tarayıcıda açar; bu hizmetin kendi gizlilik politikası geçerlidir.

Paylaşım

Verileriniz satılmaz ve reklam amacıyla paylaşılmaz. Dış hizmetler yalnızca yukarıda belirtilenlerdir: Open Food Facts, GitHub, Google ML Kit çeviri paketleri ve Apple App Store / Google Play.

Saklama ve silme

Veriler cihazdadır. Uygulamayı kaldırmak hepsini siler (Android'de ayrıca Ayarlar → Uygulamalar → Labelio → Depolama → Verileri temizle). Geliştirici tarafında saklanan kişisel veri olmadığı için silinecek ek bir kayıt yoktur.

Tıbbi uyarı

Labelio bir tıbbi cihaz değildir, tanı veya tedavi sunmaz. Sonuçlar hatalı veya eksik olabilir; kritik durumlarda her zaman ürün ambalajını ve doktorunuzun önerilerini esas alın.

Çocuklar

Uygulama çocuklara yönelik değildir; çocuklar adına ebeveynler kullanabilir.

Last updated: 14 October 2026 Responsible: Labelio developer · Contact: gunn23dev@gmail.com

In short

Labelio needs no account, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking. Your allergy and sensitivity choices, your scans and your history are stored only on your phone. As the developer we do not collect any data about you and do not send it to our servers.

What data is processed?

Data Purpose Where
Your health profile (selected allergies and sensitivities, words you add) To evaluate scans for you On your device only
Camera image To read barcodes and ingredient lists Processed on your device (Google ML Kit); not saved and not sent anywhere
Barcode number To look up the product's ingredients Sent over HTTPS to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org). Nothing that identifies you is sent; like any internet request, your IP address is visible to that service
Scan history To show the History tab On your device only (up to 100 entries)
Approximate location (optional, off by default) To add an approximate place to history entries On your device only; you can turn it off in Settings. Only approximate location is requested
Database update To fetch the latest allergen database A download from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com); no personal data is sent, your IP address is visible to GitHub
Language packs To translate labels in languages such as Chinese, Japanese or Russian With your consent, Google ML Kit translation packs are downloaded; translation runs on your device
Purchase information To activate Premium Handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; Labelio never sees your payment details

The "Other alphabet?" button opens Google Translate in your browser at your request; that service's own privacy policy applies.

Sharing

Your data is not sold and not shared for advertising. The only outside services are those listed above: Open Food Facts, GitHub, Google ML Kit translation packs and the Apple App Store / Google Play.

Storage and deletion

Data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it (on Android also Settings → Apps → Labelio → Storage → Clear data). Because the developer stores no personal data, there is no additional record to delete.

Medical notice

Labelio is not a medical device and offers no diagnosis or treatment. Results can be wrong or incomplete; in critical situations always rely on the product packaging and your doctor's advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children; parents may use it on behalf of their children.
